INFORMATION

Before you book.

OFFDAYA's launch website presents travel package information for enquiry and quotation purposes. It does not operate a real-time inventory system or accept payments.

Prices & availability

Advertised package prices are brochure-derived enquiry offers for eligible departures. Availability, exact travel dates, guest count, accommodation, services, taxes where applicable and final payable amount must be confirmed by OFFDAYA in writing before booking.

Lakshadweep

Entry permits are required. Activities and boat transfers depend on local conditions and permissions. Travel to Agatti by flight is not included in the advertised sample offer. Please see the official Lakshadweep e-permit portal.

Kashmir

Beyond Kashmir is based on a Jammu-to-Jammu itinerary. Travel to Jammu by flight or train is not included. Route, trekking and outdoor activities may change for weather, road and safety reasons.

Booking confirmation

An enquiry, WhatsApp message, quotation request or sales acceptance is not a confirmed booking. Payment instructions, cancellations, refund terms and supplier arrangements must be provided in a written OFFDAYA booking confirmation.
