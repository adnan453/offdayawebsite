INFORMATION

Privacy and enquiries.

OFFDAYA uses the information you voluntarily provide to respond to travel and stay enquiries.

What happens when you enquire

On this launch website, enquiry forms prepare a message for WhatsApp. Your details are not automatically submitted when you complete the form. You decide whether to press Send in WhatsApp. Information you send is handled through WhatsApp and used by the OFFDAYA team to answer your enquiry, prepare quotations and coordinate travel options.

Information you may share

Name, phone number, preferred travel dates, number of travellers and optional travel preferences. Please avoid sending passport numbers or other sensitive documents through the general enquiry form.

Your questions or requests

To ask about your enquiry data or request a correction or deletion, message OFFDAYA on WhatsApp at +91 79948 49989. Third-party messaging services process communications under their own terms and policies.

Images and prices

Destination images are illustrative and do not identify a confirmed hotel or resort. Prices are enquiry offers subject to date, availability and written confirmation.

Published 29 October 2026. Public policy should be reviewed alongside the final legal-operator disclosures before production launch.
